# Online Retail Orders
This project explores and analyzes data about transactions of a UK-based non-store online retail company between 01/12/2010 and 09/12/2011.\
Data source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tunguz/online-retail

---
## Initial Goals:
### Primary:
- Provide business insights and visualizations about the data and present whatever information the retail company may find valuable to boost sales and customer satisfaction.
- Build, train, and evaluate an ML model able to predict a customer's spend in the next 30 days.
### Secondary:
- Perform Market Basket Analysis to identify which products are more likely to be bought together.
- Construct product-based and customer-based analytical datasets.

---
## Project Life-cycle Phases
1. Initial exploration of the raw dataset
2. Data cleaning
3. Exploratory Data Analysis
4. Customer-based analysis
5. Product-based analysis
6. Feature Engineering
7. Model training & hyperparameter tuning
8. Market Basket Analysis
9. Final insights and recommendations
